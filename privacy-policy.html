The short version

InoBill stores the invoices, clients and business details you enter so you can use them across your devices. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you. There is no analytics or advertising software in the app.

What we store

  • The records you create: invoices, estimates, clients, business profiles, catalogue items, tax rates, payment methods and recurring schedules.
  • Your business logo and signature, if you add them.
  • An email address, only if you choose to create an account.

You can use the whole app as a guest without giving us an email address.

Where it is stored

Your records are stored on your device and in Google Cloud Firestore, part of Google Firebase, under an identifier that belongs to your account. Data is encrypted in transit. If the app cannot reach the server it keeps working and stores your records on the device.

What we never collect

  • Payment card details. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we never see your card or billing information.
  • Your location.
  • Analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers or crash-reporting profiles.

Photos

If you add a business logo, the app asks permission to open your photo library. Only the image you pick is used. We do not read any other photo.

Who else can see it

Nobody, other than Google as our hosting provider. We do not share, sell or rent your data. We disclose information only if the law requires it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app, under Menu → Account → Delete account. This removes your invoices, clients, business profiles and sign-in details permanently, and it cannot be undone. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

InoBill is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
